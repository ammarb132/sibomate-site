Privacy & Support

Private by default.

SiboMate uses a local-first core for your health history. Optional AI and backup destinations are clearly separated, consent-led, and described before use.

Last updated: September 9, 2026

The short version: your core health history is stored locally using authenticated encryption. Optional AI requests go directly to the provider and destination you choose after consent. Optional backups are encrypted. SiboMate does not route cloud AI requests through its own server.

What information the app handles

Stored locally by default

  • Your assessment answers and results, including breath-test values and symptom checks.
  • Your symptom and food logs from the Tracker.
  • Your profile details and app preferences.
  • Any AI provider API key you enter, which is kept in the secure device keychain.

This information is saved using authenticated local storage and the secure device keychain where appropriate. The local-first core is designed to keep health history on your device. Optional services are described separately below.

The research database

The app can search a database of publicly posted, community-reported experiences from health communities alongside cited research summaries. After the database is downloaded, local search can run on your device. The app may still connect to retrieve updates or use optional services.

What we do not do

  • We do not require or create a SiboMate account.
  • We do not use third-party advertising or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

AI features and your health information

The AI feature is optional and turned off until you enable it. Before you can use it, the app shows a consent screen explaining what will be shared, and you must agree.

When you ask the AI a question, the app sends your question and the context you selected, which may include assessment results, logs, and matching research excerpts, to the destination shown in the app. Apple on-device processing is available on supported iPhones. Cloud requests go directly to the provider you choose, using the API key you entered, and do not pass through our servers. The provider’s own privacy policy and terms apply. You can disconnect the AI and remove your key at any time.

AI answers are not medical advice. They can be incomplete or wrong. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making health decisions.

Purchases

SiboMate offers auto-renewing subscriptions and a one-time lifetime purchase. Purchases are processed by Apple and managed on our behalf by RevenueCat.

RevenueCat processes purchase records, receipt information, and an anonymous identifier so purchases work across reinstalls. It does not receive your name, email, or the health information you enter. Read RevenueCat’s privacy policy and Apple’s privacy policy.

Children

SiboMate is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes and your rights

You can delete local data at any time by using the app’s controls, clearing the app’s data, or uninstalling the app. If encrypted backups are enabled, delete those separately through the backup controls. If this policy changes, we will update the date above.

Support

Need help, found a bug, or have a privacy question? Email sibomate@gmail.com. We aim to respond within 2–3 business days.

Purchases and subscriptions

To restore a purchase, open the purchase screen and choose “Restore Purchases.” To cancel a subscription, open iPhone Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and does not renew.
