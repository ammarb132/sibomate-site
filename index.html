Good to know

Questions, answered
simply.

Is SiboMate medical advice?+

No. SiboMate is an educational companion. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace care from a qualified healthcare professional.

Where does my health information go?+

Your assessment, profile, symptoms, and meals stay on your device. The optional AI tool only sends information to the provider you choose with your own key.

What happens after the free trial?+

Monthly and Annual trials renew automatically through Apple unless canceled. Lifetime is a one-time purchase. If access ends, the app shows the purchase screen so you can choose a plan.

Can I use SiboMate offline?+

Yes. Once the research database is downloaded, searches run on your phone and do not require an account.
